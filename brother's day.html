🏏 12 July 2026

Rain & Cricket

Sunday, 4 pm, perfect weather. Then the sky decided to open like a bucket. 🌧️ Did we go inside? No. Aarav declared the match "not over" and bowled in the rain with a cricket bat he had named Thunder. ⚡ I stood in the garden holding an umbrella like a very serious umpire. He hit one ball into Mrs. Sharma's flower pot and immediately pointed at me and said, "Didi, you didn't see anything." 😭 We came in dripping, covered in mud, and mom just looked at us and said, "Two kids. Two baths. Now." 🏆

🎂 3 March 2026

The Secret Cake

I wanted to bake a surprise cake for mom's birthday. Aarav volunteered as "assistant chef", which meant he tasted the batter every two minutes. 🍰 By the time the oven beeped, half the batter was gone and the kitchen looked like a flour explosion. We panicked, whispered, laughed, and somehow still made a cake. It leaned a little to the left. He called it "the Tower of Pisa special." Mom cried a little and laughed a lot. Best worst cake ever. 🎉

🚲 November 2025

The Cycle Race

"Didi, race to the end of the lane?" he said, already ten steps ahead on his cycle. 🚲 I accepted, because honour was at stake. I pedalled with everything I had. He turned around, grinned, and rode with no hands on the handle to show off. Then the cycle hit a tiny stone and he wobbled straight into a hedge. 😂 He got up, brushed the leaves off, and said, "I let the hedge win." I have never laughed so hard in my life. Still undefeated. (I'm not sure that's true.) 🏁

👀 September 2025

He Always Notices

My brother acts like he never listens. Then one random evening I came home quiet after a long day, and he said nothing. He just left a cup of chai on my desk, put on my favourite old song very softly, and walked out. 🍵 No big speech. No questions. Just a small, quiet kindness. It's strange how the loudest person in the house can also be the most thoughtful one. Radar level: supernatural. 🌸

💗 Happy Brother's Day

For Aarav 🖤

Hi, little chaos machine 🌪️😂

Happy Brother's Day!! 🎉

I'll say it once, so listen carefully: thank you for being the kind of brother who turns an ordinary day into a story. Rain, burnt cakes, hedges, all of it. 😎

You think I don't notice, but I do. The way you carry responsibility quietly. The way you sense when someone needs a cup of chai instead of advice. The way you stay playful even when life gets heavy. That's rare, and I hope you know it.

I'm proud of you. Not just for the big things, but for every small, good thing you do when nobody is watching.

I know the kind of person you're going to become. The kind people respect, love, and feel safe around. And when that day comes, just remember your favourite didi called it first. 😎💗

Now stop reading and go eat something. 🍫

With all my love, your slightly unhinged but very devoted didi,
— Meera 💗
