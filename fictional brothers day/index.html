🎬 28 March 2026

Jigra & Chocolates

Exams finally over!! Chocolates distributed!! Democracy happened and we ended up watching Jigra — a movie about a sister doing everything for her brother. Very. Fitting. 😄 Now apparently leaning on walls is uncomfortable when you're a whole 5'9 giant. So naturally, the solution was to grab my arm, pull me down, and just... use me as a pillow. Excuse me?? I'm 5'2!! I'm basically travel sized!! And somehow I became the furniture. We watched the rest of the movie like the absolute chaotic family we are. 🎬🖤

👻 Republic Day

The Horror Game

Five of us. One horror game. Thirty seconds in — I had grabbed his arm and completely shut my eyes like that was a normal thing to do. 😭 He didn't move. Didn't laugh. Just stood there like a statue letting me panic in peace. Ghost passed. I opened my eyes. He turned around with the most unbothered face and said — "Miss. You are tooo weak." 😆 I scolded him. He smiled. I scolded more. He smiled MORE. I have never lost an argument so badly in my life. 💀

🖤 29 December 2025

Bindis & Bad Moods

Some days are just heavy. I was robotting through the whole day, fake smiling at everyone like a malfunctioning NPC. 😐 A reel popped up and I smiled — just for one second. This boy CAUGHT it from across the room like a sniper. 🎯 Asked what made me smile. One thing led to another and somehow — SOMEHOW — he ended up with black bindis all over his hand. 🖤 Did he complain? No. Did he try to escape? No. Did he sit there with the most resigned expression while I decorated him? Absolutely yes. Honestly the most patient little brother behavior I have ever witnessed. Someone give him an award. 🏆

👀 September 2025

He Always Notices

So this boy is 5'9. I am 5'2. He uses this information DAILY to destroy my confidence. 😭 But here's the thing nobody tells you about tall people with big egos — sometimes they're also annoyingly observant. Bad day? He knows before I open my mouth. Tired? He knows. Forcing a smile? He gives me a look that says he knows. And if I start asking my famous "but what IF—" questions?? 😂 He gives me the most gentle, carefully controlled, very-light-because-he-knows-his-own-strength playful slap and tells me to stop. 💀 He quietly asks "Didi, what happened?" while everyone else is completely oblivious. Radar level: supernatural. Patience level: also supernatural. 🌸

💗 Happy Brother's Day

For Himanshu 🖤

Hi, nimbu ka ped 🌳😂

Happy Brother's Day!! 🎉

First of all — thank you for choosing me as your elder sister, guide and teacher. Though let's be honest, I think "boss" is the more accurate title here. 😎💅

You are not just my student anymore, Himanshu. Somewhere between the horror games, the "Miss you are tooo weak" moments and the absolute chaos of knowing you — you became family. My little brother, from the heart. 🖤

I want you to know something — and listen carefully because I don't repeat myself —

You are already enough. Exactly as you are right now. The way you carry responsibility quietly without making it a show. The way you notice things others completely miss. The way you stay playful and pure even when life gets heavy. That's not common, Himanshu. That's rare.

I'm proud of you. Not just for the big things — but for every small, quiet, good thing you do every single day without anyone even noticing.

I know the kind of man you're going to become. The kind people respect naturally, love genuinely and feel safe around. And when that day comes — just remember your liliput didi called it first. 😎💗

Now. I heard you have been worried about me finding a replacement brother. 🤣 Himanshu. Seriously. WHO CHANGES THEIR BROTHER?? You are stuck with me. Permanently. No returns, no exchanges, no refunds. 😂🖤

People will say we are not blood related, so this bond has an expiry date. Let them say it. You and I both know what this is. Chosen family is still family. Brother by heart is still brother. And I promise you — no matter where life takes us, no matter how much time passes — you will always be my little brother. Always. 🌸

May Krishna ji bless you with wisdom, prosperity, good health and every single good thing this world has to offer. May you make yourself and your family proud. May you always be happy and growing. 💗

And Himanshu — if you're already planning a "Miss aap pagal hain" response —

Same. 😂🖤

With all my love, your slightly unhinged but very devoted liliput didi,
— your boss 😎💗
